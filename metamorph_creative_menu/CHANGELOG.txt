3.0.0
=====

- Added the resizable creative menu with persistent layout and assignable controls.
- Expanded spell and wand editing, including drag-and-drop, Always Cast support, undo/redo,
  appearance editing, and persistent wand presets.
- Expanded item, material, perk, effect, creature, transformation, possession, weather,
  world-rule, and teleportation tooling.
- Added bounded material painting and improved material discovery/classification.
- Improved reversible form handling, scripted attacks, held-wand forms, and return-to-human
  recovery paths.
- Improved Entangled Worlds integration for forms, items, perks, materials, weather,
  possession, boss lifecycle handling, and authority cleanup.
- Added adaptive single-player Game Over recovery for supported 32-bit Noita builds.
- Added the complete automated regression suite and deterministic source/release tooling.
